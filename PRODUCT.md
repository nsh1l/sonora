# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS with browser-native Web Audio APIs; no build dependencies for an immediately runnable visual prototype.

## Users

Assumption (the clarification round was undelivered): an individual listening to local music in a desktop browser.

## Product Purpose

An artistic audio visualizer inspired by the expressive, music-reactive feel of SoundSpectrum G-Force. Success means a user can load a local audio file and enjoy a distinctive evolving visual response.

## Positioning

A browser-native, generative visual instrument: audio-reactive forms are composed live rather than presented as a conventional equalizer.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Initial scope is a single static web surface using a user-selected local audio file and Web Audio analysis. Microphone input, external-player capture, persistence, and licensing/relationship to SoundSpectrum are undecided and excluded. This is an independent prototype, not affiliated with or a reproduction of G-Force.

## Evidence on Hand

No user-provided brand assets, audio samples, or licensing evidence. Any demo track must be selected by the user.

## Product Principles

- Let the visual work lead.
- Make loading and playback obvious.
- Keep audio local to the browser session.
- Do not imply affiliation with or copy proprietary G-Force assets.
