SONORA

Privacy

SONORA is a visual instrument. This page describes the app and website as prepared in October 2026.

Local audio and microphone

Audio files you choose are played and analyzed on your device. SONORA does not upload those files. The iPhone microphone source analyzes frequency bands on your device; it does not record or upload microphone audio. You can deny microphone access or stop that source.

Music services

Apple Music mode reads the current item and playback position to make procedural art. It does not capture the music stream or analyze its audio. Optional Spotify mode is hidden until a developer configures it. If enabled and you choose to connect, Spotify receives authorization and metadata requests. SONORA keeps the access token in memory while connected and clears it on disconnect.

Website and storage

The website caches application files for offline reopening. It does not cache selected audio. The optional sample track is requested from our host only when you choose it. SONORA does not include analytics or advertising SDKs. The website host may process ordinary request logs under its own policies.

Questions or privacy requests: contact support through GitHub Issues. GitHub processes information you submit there under its own policies.
