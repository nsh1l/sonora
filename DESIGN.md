# Design system — SONORA

SONORA treats playback as a small performance: the visualization owns the field, while controls recede into a precise instrument rail.

- **Palette:** near-black blue `#080b12`, elevated control surface `#111722`, quiet borders `#293342`, clear text `#f2f3f4`, mint action `#b6f5d4`, coral status `#ff917b`, and pale blue `#98b8ff`.
- **Typography:** native system sans for clarity; large, lightly weighted, tightly tracked display text. Small uppercase labels use generous tracking only for short states.
- **Materials:** layered translucent ink planes and fine spectral lines over a deep field, without decorative glow in Bloom/Orbit/Tide; Dub deliberately uses saturated glow and feedback residue. Datamosh uses torn pigment planes in charcoal teal, oxide coral, acid olive and muted lilac, with block-based previous-frame reconstruction rather than fine-line geometry. Cymatic adds granular nodal printwork: a tilted standing-wave plate in chalk, vermilion and cobalt misregistration, without geometry traces or stored particle histories. No cards, glass panels, or gradient text. Dice adds solid shaded chalk cubes, one vermilion die and dark fixed pips, with blue treble registration along their edges.
- **Composition:** slim identity bar, expansive artwork with a lower-left quiet zone for the welcome text, and one bottom control rail. At narrow widths the rail becomes a compact stacked layout.
- **Motion:** seven distinct visual instruments: Bloom's layered geometric petals and fine radial veins, Orbit's woven Lissajous trajectories and orbiting points, Tide's horizontal contour strata, and Dub's thick feedback ribbons with three time-delayed echoes and transient RGB displacement. Datamosh displaces held previous-frame macroblocks and horizontal slices; pigment refreshes follow 40–250 Hz rising events (hysteresis and cooldown), not a repeating timer; held notes do not repeatedly inject shapes. Cymatic redistributes powder along an analytical nodal field, with bass/mid-driven modes and treble-driven blue registration offset; its tilt and interference phase freeze under reduced motion. Dice arranges seven varied, orthographically projected cubes as a fitted floating sculpture; fixed face-local pips and opposing totals of seven remain attached through rotation, back-face culling and far-to-near painting. Bass adds lift/scale, mids tilt and treble offsets the blue edge. Reduced motion freezes tumbling, mid tilt and bounce while retaining live scale/registration; Apple playback-position art is deterministic and holds when paused. No physics, BPM or beat-analysis claim is implied. Dub disables feedback motion and RGB displacement under reduced motion; Datamosh disables temporal reconstruction entirely. Neither uses full-screen strobe. Bass expands the field, mids change its shape, and treble roughens fine details. Reduced-motion preference freezes phase movement while allowing audio response. Audio starts only after the user selects a file and presses play.
- **Controls:** native file picker and select; large central play/pause; explicit restart, seek, and skip-to-end actions; visible ready, playing, paused, and error states. Native iPhone Visual style uses a menu for all seven styles; the Source picker remains segmented.

This visual identity is an original interpretation of an artistic audio visualizer, not a copy of SoundSpectrum G-Force or its proprietary assets.
