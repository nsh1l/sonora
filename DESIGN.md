# Design system — SONORA

SONORA treats playback as a small performance: the visualization owns the field, while controls recede into a precise instrument rail.

- **Palette:** near-black blue `#080b12`, elevated control surface `#111722`, quiet borders `#293342`, clear text `#f2f3f4`, mint action `#b6f5d4`, coral status `#ff917b`, and pale blue `#98b8ff`.
- **Typography:** native system sans for clarity; large, lightly weighted, tightly tracked display text. Small uppercase labels use generous tracking only for short states.
- **Materials:** layered translucent ink planes and fine spectral lines over a deep field, without decorative glow in Bloom/Orbit/Tide; Dub deliberately uses saturated glow and feedback residue. Datamosh uses torn pigment planes in charcoal teal, oxide coral, acid olive and muted lilac, with block-based previous-frame reconstruction rather than fine-line geometry. No cards, glass panels, or gradient text.
- **Composition:** slim identity bar, expansive artwork with a lower-left quiet zone for the welcome text, and one bottom control rail. At narrow widths the rail becomes a compact stacked layout.
- **Motion:** five distinct visual instruments: Bloom's unfolding petals, Orbit's woven Lissajous trajectories, Tide's horizontal contour strata, and Dub's thick feedback ribbons with three time-delayed echoes and transient RGB displacement. Datamosh displaces held previous-frame macroblocks and horizontal slices; partial pigment refreshes prevent a field from becoming a uniform smear. Dub disables feedback motion and RGB displacement under reduced motion; Datamosh disables temporal reconstruction entirely. Neither uses full-screen strobe. Bass expands the field, mids change its shape, and treble roughens fine details. Reduced-motion preference freezes phase movement while allowing audio response. Audio starts only after the user selects a file and presses play.
- **Controls:** native file picker and select; large central play/pause; explicit restart, seek, and skip-to-end actions; visible ready, playing, paused, and error states.

This visual identity is an original interpretation of an artistic audio visualizer, not a copy of SoundSpectrum G-Force or its proprietary assets.
