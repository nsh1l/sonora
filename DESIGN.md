# Design system — SONORA

SONORA treats playback as a small performance: the visualization owns the field, while controls recede into a precise instrument rail.

- **Palette:** near-black blue `#080b12`, elevated control surface `#111722`, quiet borders `#293342`, clear text `#f2f3f4`, mint action `#b6f5d4`, coral status `#ff917b`, and pale blue `#98b8ff`.
- **Typography:** native system sans for clarity; large, lightly weighted, tightly tracked display text. Small uppercase labels use generous tracking only for short states.
- **Materials:** layered translucent ink planes and fine spectral lines over a deep field, without decorative glow in Bloom/Orbit/Tide; Dub deliberately uses saturated glow and feedback residue. Datamosh uses torn pigment planes in charcoal teal, oxide coral, acid olive and muted lilac, with block-based previous-frame reconstruction rather than fine-line geometry. Cymatic adds granular nodal printwork: a viewport-filling, uniformly scaled standing-wave field in chalk, vermilion and cobalt misregistration, without geometry traces or stored particle histories. No cards, glass panels, or gradient text. Dice uses nearly transparent teal/coral cube planes with fine mint/coral contours and projected pip rings, plus a single faint pale-blue treble edge echo; no opaque chalk, dark spots or glow.
- **Composition:** slim identity bar, expansive artwork with a lower-left quiet zone for the welcome text, and one bottom control rail. At narrow widths the rail becomes a compact stacked layout.
- **Motion:** seven distinct visual instruments: Bloom's layered geometric petals and fine radial veins, Orbit's woven Lissajous trajectories and orbiting points, Tide's horizontal contour strata, and Dub's thick feedback ribbons with three time-delayed echoes and transient RGB displacement. Datamosh displaces held previous-frame macroblocks and horizontal slices; pigment refreshes follow 40–250 Hz rising events (hysteresis and cooldown), not a repeating timer; held notes do not repeatedly inject shapes. Cymatic redistributes powder along an analytical nodal field, with bass/mid-driven modes and treble-driven blue registration offset; its tilt and interference phase freeze under reduced motion. Dice arranges seven varied, orthographically projected cubes as a fitted floating sculpture; fixed face-local pips and opposing totals of seven remain attached through rotation, back-face culling and far-to-near painting. Bass adds lift/scale, mids tilt and treble offsets the pale-blue echo. Reduced motion freezes tumbling, mid tilt and bounce while retaining live scale/registration; Apple playback-position art is deterministic and holds when paused. No physics, BPM or beat-analysis claim is implied. Dub disables feedback motion and RGB displacement under reduced motion; Datamosh disables temporal reconstruction entirely. Neither uses full-screen strobe. Bass expands the field, mids change its shape, and treble roughens fine details. Reduced-motion preference freezes phase movement while allowing audio response. Audio starts only after the user selects a file and presses play.
- **Fullscreen guidance:** faint, safe-area-aware bottom text appears only on entry, then glitches and fades out within five seconds; pointer events pass through. Reduced motion uses a plain fade.
- **Controls:** native file picker and select; large central play/pause; explicit restart, seek, and skip-to-end actions; visible ready, playing, paused, and error states. Native iPhone Visual style uses a menu for all seven styles; the Source picker remains segmented.

This visual identity is an original interpretation of an artistic audio visualizer, not a copy of SoundSpectrum G-Force or its proprietary assets.
